What we store

Your account details (name, email, mobile), your identity verification details, your recipients and your transfer history. We don't store card numbers: only the last four digits are kept for display.

How it's protected

  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • The keys to each recipient's wallet are encrypted, and the encryption key is kept separately from the database.
  • Wallet keys are never shown on any page or sent in any response.
  • Sessions are signed and expire after a few hours.

Who can see it

Only you and the platform's administrators, who need it to review identity checks and confirm payments. Transfers on the XRPL Testnet are public by design: anyone can see a transaction's amount and wallet addresses, but not your name.
