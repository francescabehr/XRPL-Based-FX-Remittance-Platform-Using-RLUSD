What we store

Your account details (name, email, mobile), your identity verification details, your recipients and your transfer history. We don't store card numbers.

How it's protected

    We ensure all sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we limit access to it to the minimum number of people who need it.

Who can see it

Only you and the platform's administrators, who need it to review identity checks and confirm payments. Transfers on the XRPL Testnet are public by design: anyone can see a transaction's amount and wallet addresses, but not your name.

If something goes wrong

If we learn that personal data has been accessed without permission, we'll let affected users know by email as soon as we can, along with what happened and what we're doing about it.

A note on testing

Orbyt is a student prototype, not a production system. We take reasonable care, but we recommend using test details wherever you can rather than real identity documents.
