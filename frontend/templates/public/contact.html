When we reply

We check messages Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 (SAST), and aim to reply within two working days. Replies may be slower over holiday periods.

Before you write

If your message is about a transfer, include its reference from the transfer's status page and the email address on your account. That lets us find it without asking you again.

Never send us your password or card details; we will never ask for them. If someone claiming to be from Orbyt does, please let us know.
