What we built

A sender in South Africa pays in rand. Once the payment is confirmed, the recipient receives UCTUSD, a dollar test token, in a wallet on the XRPL Testnet. The recipient can then cash out to USD or ZAR.

Every transfer leaves a transaction hash that anyone can look up in the public XRPL explorer, so each step can be checked independently.

Why the XRPL

The XRP Ledger settles payments in a few seconds at very low cost, and it supports tokens issued by a trusted party. That makes it a good fit for testing a remittance flow end to end.

Who we are

Orbyt is a class project built by a small student team. It is a prototype for learning and demonstration, not a licensed financial service, and it never handles real money.
