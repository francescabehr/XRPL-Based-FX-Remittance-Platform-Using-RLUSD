Getting started

  1. Create an account with your email and mobile number.
  2. Verify your identity. An admin reviews your details before you can send.
  3. Add a recipient, enter an amount in rand and review the quote.
  4. Pay with the simulated card, then follow the transfer on its status page.

FAQs

Is this real money?

No. Card payments, exchange rates and fiat payouts are simulated, and UCTUSD is a test token on the XRPL Testnet with no real value. Please don't enter real card details.

How long does verification take?

An admin reviews each submission by hand, usually within a working day. You can check its status under Verification after you log in.

Why can't I send money yet?

Sending is switched on once your identity verification is approved. If it's still pending, it hasn't been reviewed yet; if it was declined, the Verification page shows what to do next.

Is there a limit on how much I can send?

Yes. Sending limits apply to every account, as they would with a real remittance service. If a transfer goes over your limit, the quote page will tell you before you pay.

How long does a transfer take?

Once an admin confirms your payment, settlement on the XRPL Testnet usually takes a few seconds. The status page updates on its own.

How can I check a transfer on the ledger?

Each completed transfer shows a transaction hash on its status page. Paste it into the public XRPL Testnet explorer to see the payment, its amount and the wallets involved.

How do I cash out?

Recipients choose Cash out in their wallet, enter an amount and pick USD or ZAR. The UCTUSD is burned on the ledger and the payout is simulated.

I forgot my password

Password resets aren't available in this prototype yet. Contact us from the email on your account and we'll help.

Still stuck?

Contact us with your transfer reference if you have one, and we'll get back to you within two working days.
