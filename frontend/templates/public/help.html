Getting started

  • Create an account with your email and mobile number.
  • Verify your identity. An admin reviews your details before you can send.
  • Add a recipient, enter an amount in rand and review the quote.
  • Pay with the simulated card, then follow the transfer on its status page.

FAQs

Is this real money?

No. Card payments, exchange rates and fiat payouts are simulated, and UCTUSD is a test token on the XRPL Testnet with no real value.

How long does a transfer take?

Once an admin confirms your payment, settlement on the XRPL Testnet usually takes a few seconds. The status page updates on its own.

Why can't I send money yet?

Sending is switched on once your identity verification is approved. You can check its status under Verification after you log in.

How do I cash out?

Recipients choose Cash out in their wallet, enter an amount and pick USD or ZAR. The UCTUSD is burned on the ledger and the payout is simulated.

I forgot my password

Password resets aren't available in this prototype. Contact us and we'll help.
