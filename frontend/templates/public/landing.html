How it works

  1. Step 1

    Sign up and verify

    Create an account and submit your KYC details. An admin reviews and approves them before you can send.

  2. Step 2

    Pay in rand

    Choose a recipient, see the exact quote, and pay with a simulated card. An admin confirms the payment.

  3. Step 3

    Settled on XRPL

    UCTUSD moves to the recipient's own Testnet wallet. Every transfer has a hash you can check in the XRPL explorer.

  4. Step 4

    Cash out

    The recipient cashes out to USD or ZAR. The UCTUSD is burned on the ledger and the payout is simulated.

What's real, what's simulated

Real, on the XRPL Testnet

  • Payments from the platform treasury to each recipient's wallet
  • Trust lines to the UCTUSD issuer
  • Cash-out burns back to the issuer
  • Transaction hashes you can open in the explorer

Simulated

  • Card payments (no real card network)
  • The ZAR/USD market rate, which an admin sets
  • Fiat payouts in USD and ZAR
  • UCTUSD itself is a test token with no real value
