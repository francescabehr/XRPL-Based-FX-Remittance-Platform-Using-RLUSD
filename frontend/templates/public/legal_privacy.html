Last updated: 27 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Identity verification: date of birth, nationality, ID or passport number, address and source of funds.
  • Recipients you add, and the transfers and cash-outs you make.

Because Orbyt is a prototype, we encourage you to use test details for identity verification rather than your real ID number.

Why we use it

To run your account, check your identity before you can send, apply sending limits, and show you the status of your transfers. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We don't share your personal information with third parties. The only thing that leaves the platform is ledger activity: wallet addresses and amounts are recorded on the public XRPL Testnet, without your name.

How long we keep it

For as long as the prototype runs. Identity verification records are kept unchanged as the record of what was checked. When the project ends, its data will be deleted.

Your choices

You can update your email, mobile and password from your profile. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or for it to be deleted, email privacy@orbyt.com from the address on your account. We'll respond within two working days.

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives people these rights over their data. Orbyt is a student project rather than a registered business, but we aim to respect them in the same way.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and the date above.
