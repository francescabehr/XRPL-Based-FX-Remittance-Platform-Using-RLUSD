What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Identity verification: date of birth, nationality, ID or passport number, address and source of funds.
  • Recipients you add, and the transfers and cash-outs you make.

Why we use it

To run your account, check your identity before you can send, apply sending limits, and show you the status of your transfers. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as the prototype runs. Identity verification records are kept unchanged as the record of what was checked.

Your choices

You can update your email, mobile and password from your profile. To ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, email privacy@example.com.
