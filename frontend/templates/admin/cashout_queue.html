Reconcile resolves a burn whose outcome was never confirmed. It checks the XRPL directly: a validated success completes the request, and a burn proven never to have been included (past its LastLedgerSequence, with unbroken ledger history) fails it and returns the reserved UCTUSD. If neither can be proven, the reserve stays held and nothing changes.
